Your Global Supply Chain Partner

International Logistics is the latest addition to Ashtam Global Logistics' expanding portfolio. Building on our proven domestic capabilities, we now extend our expertise to global supply chains — delivering seamless, end-to-end freight solutions across international markets.

We manage the full logistics lifecycle: ocean freight, air freight, ODC road movements, customs clearance, and warehousing. Our process-driven execution and trusted global partner network ensure regulatory compliance, cost efficiency, and on-schedule delivery for every international shipment.

From FCL containers to project cargo, from standard commercial freight to hazardous goods — we handle complex international logistics so your business can scale without borders.

Our International Service Scope

End-to-end global logistics solutions from origin to destination

Ocean Freight (FCL / LCL)

Full container and less-than-container load shipments via major international shipping lines to global ports.

Air Freight

Time-critical international shipments via air cargo for high-value, perishable, or urgent consignments worldwide.

ODC Road Movements

Over-dimensional and heavy cargo road transport for cross-border and inland movements to ports and warehouses.

Customs Clearance

End-to-end customs documentation, compliance, duty management, and clearance at all major Indian ports and airports.

Warehousing & Distribution

International-grade warehousing, bonded storage, and distribution services for import/export cargo management.

Project Cargo Logistics

Complex, multi-modal project cargo handling for large-scale industrial and infrastructure projects across borders.

Dangerous Goods (DG)

Certified handling and transport of hazardous materials in full compliance with IATA, IMDG, and ADR regulations.

Multimodal Solutions

Integrated sea-air, road-sea, and air-road solutions optimized for cost, transit time, and cargo requirements.

Door-to-Door Delivery

Complete origin-to-destination service covering pickup, freight, customs, and final delivery at the consignee's address.

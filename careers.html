Why Work at Ashtam Global Logistics?

At Ashtam Global Logistics, we believe our people are our greatest strength. We are building a fast-growing, innovation-driven logistics company that tackles India's most complex transportation challenges — and we need talented, driven individuals to grow with us.

Join a team that works on high-impact projects across power, infrastructure, renewables, and heavy engineering sectors. Here, your work matters — every project you support powers India's industrial growth.

We offer a collaborative work environment, clear growth paths, continuous learning opportunities, and the satisfaction of solving real-world logistics challenges every day.

Perks & Benefits

Collaborative Culture

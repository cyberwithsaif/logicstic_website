Proven Track Record of Excellence

Discover how we have consistently delivered on complex logistical challenges, ensuring our clients' projects stay on track, on time, and on budget.

Power Sector • 2025

220kV Transformer Transport Across Hilly Terrain

The Challenge

Transporting a massive 180-ton 220kV power transformer from a manufacturing facility in Central India to a remote substation situated in a mountainous region with weak bridges and sharp hairpin bends.

The Solution

We deployed a 16-axle hydraulic modular trailer equipped with heavy-duty prime movers. Our engineering team constructed temporary bridge bypasses and utilized a push-pull configuration to navigate steep gradients securely.

The Result

The transformer was delivered safely and positioned flawlessly onto its plinth 5 days ahead of the scheduled commissioning deadline, saving the client significant downtime costs.

Oil & Gas • 2024

Oversized Distillation Column Delivery

The Challenge

Moving a 45-meter long distillation column weighing 120 tons from a western port to an inland refinery. The route required traversing multiple state borders and navigating urban congestion.

The Solution

Conducted extensive swept-path analyses to negotiate urban corners. Coordinated with local authorities to lift overhead power lines and scheduled movement strictly during night-time hours to minimize public disruption.

The Result

Seamless cross-border transit with zero safety incidents. The refinery expansion project remained on schedule, highlighting our proficiency in regulatory compliance and route management.

Manufacturing • Ongoing

Pan-India Contract Logistics Optimization

The Challenge

A major heavy equipment manufacturer faced erratic delivery schedules and escalating freight costs due to uncoordinated ad-hoc transport arrangements across their 4 manufacturing plants.

The Solution

Implemented a comprehensive Contract Logistics model. Deployed a dedicated fleet of 30+ vehicles tailored to their product dimensions, backed by a centralized digital tracking system and customized SOPs.

The Result

Achieved a 98.5% on-time delivery rate within the first 6 months and reduced overall logistics expenditure by 14% through optimized capacity utilization.

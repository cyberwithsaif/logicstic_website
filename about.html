Who We Are

Excellence in Heavy Logistics

Ashtam Global Logistics Pvt. Ltd. is a dynamic startup logistics company committed to delivering reliable, efficient, and customized logistics solutions for complex and high-value cargo. Though young in establishment, our team brings deep industry expertise and operational knowledge across multiple critical sectors.

We specialize in end-to-end logistics solutions for Defence, Power Transformers, Hydro Projects, Oil & Gas, Energy, Infrastructure, Renewables, and Heavy Engineering industries. Our strength lies in handling over-dimensional, heavy, and project cargo, ensuring precision, safety, and timely execution at every stage.

Driven by professionalism, transparency, and a customer-first approach, we aim to build long-term partnerships by consistently delivering dependable logistics support. As we grow, our focus remains on innovation, operational excellence, and becoming a trusted logistics partner for India's core and emerging industries.

Our Journey

How We Became a Trusted Logistics Partner

From a bold vision to India's emerging project logistics specialist — here is the story of our growth, milestones, and commitment to excellence.

Jan 2022

Company Founded

Ashtam Global Logistics Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated in Gurugram, Haryana, with a clear mission to deliver precision-driven project logistics for India's industrial sector.

Mid 2022

First Major Project Delivery

Successfully executed our first large-scale power transformer transportation project — setting the benchmark for safety, regulatory compliance, and on-time delivery.

Aug 2022

UDYAM Registration Achieved

Officially registered under the Government of India's MSME/UDYAM scheme (UDYAM-HR-05-0028054), establishing our credentials as a recognized Indian enterprise.

2023

ISO 9001:2015 Certification

Achieved ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System Certification — demonstrating our commitment to consistent service excellence and strict quality standards across all operations.

2023

Oil & Gas Sector Expansion

Extended our capabilities into Oil & Gas and Energy sectors, handling specialized pipeline equipment and heavy machinery logistics across remote and industrial corridors.

Nov 2023

DPIIT Startup Recognition

Awarded the prestigious DPIIT Certificate of Recognition (DIPP145877) by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade, Government of India — validating our innovation-driven approach.

2024

International Logistics Division Launched

Expanded our portfolio to include international freight services — Ocean Freight, Air Freight, Customs Clearance, and ODC Road Movement — opening global supply chain capabilities for our clients.

2025–Present

Pan-India Operations & Growing

Now operating across India's major industrial corridors with a capacity range from 1 MT to 240 MT. Proudly serving renewable energy, infrastructure, defence, and heavy engineering sectors with a growing fleet and dedicated team.

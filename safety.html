Safety Philosophy

Safety is Our Highest Priority

At Ashtam Global Logistics, safety is not a procedure — it is a core value embedded in our culture. Every movement we plan and execute is governed by rigorous Health, Safety, and Environmental (HSE) standards designed to protect our people, your cargo, third parties, and the environment.

We comply with all applicable MoRTH regulations, IRC norms, state transport authority requirements, and project-specific HSE plans. Our zero-incident commitment is not just a goal — it is the standard by which we measure every operation.

Our Protocols

HSE Management System

Our comprehensive HSE management system covers every phase of logistics operations — from pre-movement planning to final delivery.

Route Surveys & Feasibility

Physical and GIS-based route surveys covering road geometry, gradients, turning radius, overhead clearances, bridges, culverts, ROBs, and utility crossings before any movement begins.

Risk Assessment & Mitigation

Pre-movement risk identification, assessment, and mitigation planning — including contingency routes, load stability analysis, and weather condition monitoring for every project.

PPE & Personnel Safety

Mandatory use of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) by all on-ground personnel. Toolbox talks conducted before every major lift and movement operation.

OEM-Compliant Load Securing

Engineered loading, lashing design, load restraint systems, and vibration control using OEM-compliant methods for high-value transformers and heavy machinery.

Escort & Traffic Management

Dedicated pilot vehicles, route marshals, police escort coordination, night-movement management, and controlled traffic diversion where required.

Emergency Response Planning

Documented emergency response procedures covering accident management, equipment breakdown, load shift contingency, and immediate stakeholder communication protocols.

Safety Training & Awareness

Regular safety induction training for all operational staff, driver briefings, and site-specific safety orientation before each project assignment.

Regulatory Compliance

End-to-end coordination for RTO permits, police permissions, highway authority clearances, forest department approvals, DISCOM coordination, and local administrative compliance.

Our Zero-Incident Commitment

Every project we undertake is executed with the unwavering goal of zero incidents, zero injuries, and zero damage to cargo, personnel, or the environment. This is not a target — it is the minimum standard we demand of ourselves on every assignment, no matter the complexity or scale.

How We Manage Risk

Risk Management Process

Our structured risk management approach ensures that every potential hazard is identified, assessed, and mitigated before operations begin.

1

Identify Hazards

Route surveys, site inspections, cargo analysis, and historical data review to identify all potential risks.

2

Assess Risk Level

Severity and likelihood matrix to prioritize risks and determine acceptable risk thresholds for each operation.

3

Mitigate & Control

Engineering controls, administrative procedures, PPE requirements, and contingency route planning to eliminate or reduce risk.

4

Monitor & Report

Real-time on-ground supervision, daily progress reporting, and post-delivery incident/near-miss review for continuous improvement.

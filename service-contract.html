Contract Logistics

Contract Integrated Logistics Services

At Ashtam Global Logistics Pvt. Ltd., we provide comprehensive contract-based, end-to-end integrated logistics solutions to multinational companies, EPC contractors, and large industrial clients, covering both inbound and outbound logistics operations.

Our service framework is designed to support diverse cargo profiles and vehicle capacities ranging from 1 MT to 40 MT, ensuring flexibility across light commercial, medium, and heavy-duty transportation requirements. Solutions are custom-engineered to align with project workflows, production cycles, and supply chain objectives.

Through long-term logistics contracts of up to two years, we ensure operational continuity, capacity assurance, cost optimization, and service consistency. Our contract logistics model enables clients to benefit from dedicated fleets, standardized operating procedures, performance monitoring, and a single-point coordination structure.

With a strong focus on compliance, reliability, and process-driven execution, Ashtam Global Logistics delivers sustainable, scalable, and value-driven logistics partnerships that support long-term business growth.

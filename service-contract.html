Integrated Supply Chain Under One Contract

We provide comprehensive contract-based, end-to-end logistics solutions for multinational companies, EPC contractors, and large industrial clients — covering both inbound and outbound logistics operations with dedicated fleets, standardized SOPs, and a single-point coordination structure.

Our contract model supports cargo from 1 MT to 40 MT, with long-term agreements up to two years, ensuring operational continuity, capacity assurance, cost optimization, and service consistency aligned with your production cycles and project timelines.

What's Included in Contract Logistics

A complete, managed logistics ecosystem built around your operational needs — from fleet to warehouse to real-time reporting.

Dedicated Fleet Management

Assigned vehicles calibrated to your cargo profile — light commercial, medium, and heavy-duty — with scheduled maintenance and driver compliance.

Warehouse & Inventory Control

Managed warehouse operations including inbound receipt, inventory tracking, order picking, and outbound dispatch aligned with production schedules.

JIT Delivery Management

Just-in-time delivery schedules synchronized with your plant production cycles to eliminate inventory build-up and reduce holding costs.

Multi-Site Coordination

Centralized dispatch and operations coordination across multiple plant locations, warehouses, and project sites under one contract.

Cost Optimization Programs

Route optimization, load consolidation, fuel efficiency programs, and quarterly business reviews to drive continuous logistics cost reduction.

MIS Reporting & Analytics

Monthly performance dashboards covering delivery adherence, fleet utilization, cost metrics, and SLA compliance for full operational visibility.

Dedicated Account Manager

A single-point senior account manager responsible for your contract — escalations, planning, reporting, and continuous improvement initiatives.

Compliance & Safety

Driver training, vehicle fitness, statutory permit management, cargo insurance, and full HSSE compliance across all contract operations.

Scalable Capacity

Flexible fleet scaling based on seasonal demand, project peaks, or capacity ramp-ups — without long-term lock-in on vehicle count.

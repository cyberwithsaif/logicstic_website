End-to-End ODC Logistics for Power Transformers

Ashtam Global Logistics delivers specialized, end-to-end transportation for oversized and heavy power transformers — supporting EPC contractors, OEMs, utilities (PGCIL, NTPC, MNRE), and state electricity boards with precision-engineered execution and zero-incident commitment.

Every movement is backed by physical route surveys, statutory permits, axle load engineering, and a dedicated on-ground operations team — ensuring your critical equipment reaches its destination safely and on schedule.

Full Scope

Complete Scope of Services

Every service in our transformer transportation portfolio is engineered for safety, compliance, and precision — from planning to final placement.

Route Survey & Feasibility

Physical and GIS-based surveys covering road geometry, gradients, bridge ratings, overhead clearances, culverts, and utility crossings.

Axle Load Engineering

Axle distribution planning, trailer configuration, load spreading calculations, and hydraulic/modular axle system selection per IRC & MoRTH norms.

Statutory Permits & Compliance

End-to-end RTO permits, police permissions, highway authority NOCs, forest department clearances, and DISCOM coordination.

Movement Planning & Scheduling

Detailed methodology, risk assessment, contingency planning, and alignment with EPC project milestones and commissioning timelines.

Specialized Equipment Deployment

Heavy-duty hydraulic axle trailers, prime movers, pullers, pushers, and customized load handling per transformer specifications.

Engineered Loading & Lashing

OEM-compliant loading methods, lashing design, load restraint systems, and vibration control for high-value transformers.

Escort & Traffic Management

Pilot vehicles, route marshals, police escorts, night-movement coordination, and controlled traffic diversion where required.

Utility Shifting & Route Prep

Temporary shifting of overhead lines, signage removal, road strengthening, ramp creation, and bridge protection works.

Controlled Transit & Monitoring

Speed-controlled movement, real-time GPS coordination, on-ground supervision, and daily progress reporting to client.

Last-Mile & Site Handling

Final approach planning, site entry management, crane rigging support, and precise placement at foundation or storage location.

HSE & Risk Management

Project HSE plan compliance, toolbox talks, safety audits, emergency response planning, and zero-incident execution commitment.

Documentation & Reporting

Complete movement records, permit copies, photographic evidence, and post-delivery MIS report delivered to client team.

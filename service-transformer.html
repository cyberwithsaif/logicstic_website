Transformer Transportation

Transformer Transportation Services

At Ashtam Global Logistics Pvt. Ltd., we deliver end-to-end logistics solutions for oversized and heavy power transformers, supporting EPC contractors, OEMs, and utilities across India with precision-driven execution.

Scope of Services

  • Detailed Route Survey & Feasibility Analysis: Physical and GIS-based route surveys covering road geometry, gradients, turning radius, overhead clearances, bridges, culverts, ROBs, and utility crossings.
  • Axle Load & Transport Engineering: Axle load distribution, trailer configuration planning, load spreading calculations, and selection of hydraulic/modular axle systems in line with IRC & MoRTH norms.
  • Statutory Permissions & Regulatory Compliance: End-to-end coordination for RTO permits, police permissions, highway authorities, forest departments, DISCOMs, and local administrative bodies.
  • Movement Planning & Scheduling: Detailed movement methodology, risk assessment, contingency planning, and alignment with EPC project milestones.
  • Specialized Equipment Deployment: Deployment of heavy-duty hydraulic axle trailers, prime movers, pullers, pushers, and customized load handling arrangements as per transformer specifications.
  • Engineered Loading, Lashing & Securing: OEM-compliant loading methods, lashing design, load restraint systems, and vibration control for high-value transformers.
  • Escort & Traffic Management: Pilot vehicles, route marshals, police escorts, night-movement coordination, and controlled traffic diversion where required.
  • Utility Shifting & Route Preparation: Temporary shifting of overhead lines, signage removal, road strengthening, ramp creation, and bridge protection works.
  • Controlled Transit & Monitoring: Speed-controlled movement, real-time coordination, on-ground supervision, and daily progress reporting.
  • Last-Mile Delivery & Site Handling: Final approach planning, site entry management, unloading support, and placement at foundation or storage location.
  • HSE & Risk Management: Compliance with project HSE plans, toolbox talks, safety audits, emergency response planning, and zero-incident execution.

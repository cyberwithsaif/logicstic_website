Flexible, On-Demand Freight Solutions

At Ashtam Global Logistics, our General Logistics service is built for speed and adaptability. We handle urgent, short-notice, and just-in-time cargo movements that require rapid mobilization, reliable execution, and pan-India reach — from industrial plants to remote project sites.

Whether it's a one-time spot movement or a recurring requirement outside your contract agreement, our experienced operations team ensures quick allocation, optimal routing, and safe, on-time delivery.

Backed by a wide fleet network and real-time coordination capabilities, we are the trusted logistics partner for companies that can't afford delays.

What We Handle

Comprehensive general logistics solutions for every cargo requirement

Spot & Urgent Transport

Same-day and emergency freight allocation for time-critical shipments with no advance notice required.

Just-in-Time Deliveries

Precision-timed deliveries to manufacturing plants, warehouses, and project sites — zero buffer, zero disruption.

Inbound & Outbound Movements

End-to-end freight management for industrial and commercial cargo — both incoming raw materials and outgoing products.

Full Truck Load (FTL)

Dedicated trucks for large volume shipments with direct origin-to-destination routing for maximum speed.

Part Load (PTL)

Cost-efficient part-load shipments for smaller consignments consolidated with other compatible cargo.

High-Priority Consignments

Expedited handling for critical, high-value, or time-sensitive cargo with dedicated operations support.

Remote Area Coverage

Pan-India reach including constrained locations, remote sites, and areas with limited infrastructure access.

Real-Time Coordination

Live tracking, driver communication, and operations desk support throughout every movement.

Statutory Compliance

All movements executed in full compliance with road transport regulations, permits, and safety standards.

General Logistics Services

At Ashtam Global Logistics Pvt. Ltd., our General Logistics services are designed to efficiently manage urgent, short-notice, and just-in-time (JIT) cargo movements that fall outside long-term or contract-based logistics agreements.

We specialize in handling:

  • Spot and urgent transportation requirements
  • Just-in-time (JIT) deliveries to plants, warehouses, and project sites
  • Inbound and outbound movements for industrial and commercial cargo
  • Full Truck Load (FTL) and Part Load (PTL) shipments
  • Time-critical and high-priority consignments
  • Movements across pan-India locations, including remote and constrained areas

Supported by a reliable fleet network and experienced operations team, we ensure quick mobilization, route optimization, real-time coordination, and safe delivery while maintaining compliance with statutory and safety standards.

Our flexible operating model enables clients to respond swiftly to dynamic supply chain demands without compromising on reliability or service quality.

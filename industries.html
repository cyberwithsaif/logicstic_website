Industries We Serve

Empowering the Backbone of Global Infrastructure

Different industries face vastly different logistical challenges. Ashtam Global Logistics brings specialized knowledge, equipment, and experience to address the unique needs of India's most critical sectors.

Defence & Aerospace

Security, precision, and absolute confidentiality are paramount when serving the defence sector. We handle the transportation of sensitive equipment, heavy machinery, and critical supplies for military bases and aerospace manufacturing facilities across the nation.

  • Secure and confidential transport protocols.
  • Specialized handling of out-of-gauge military hardware.

Power & Transformers

The power generation and transmission sector relies on massive, heavy components. We are undisputed experts in the transportation of power transformers, generators, stators, and turbines to remote substations and power plants.

  • Route planning for extreme weight loads.
  • Substation positioning and multi-modal logistics.

Oil & Gas

Logistics for the Oil & Gas sector involves navigating hazardous environments and handling ultra-heavy refining equipment. We provide robust transport solutions for pressure vessels, columns, rigs, and pipeline materials.

  • Strict adherence to HSSE standards.
  • Delivery to remote and challenging terrains.

Infrastructure & EPC

Building the nation's infrastructure requires an uninterrupted supply chain. We partner with leading Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) firms to deliver heavy construction equipment, structural steel, and precast concrete.

  • Synchronized JIT deliveries for active construction sites.
  • Transport of cranes and earthmoving machinery.

Renewables

The transition to green energy involves transporting oversized wind turbine blades, nacelles, and solar farm equipment. We offer specialized logistics solutions tailored to the unique dimensions of renewable energy components.

  • Specialized trailers for ultra-long wind blades.
  • High-volume logistics for solar project rollouts.

End-to-end logistics solutions for heavy, over-dimensional, and high-value cargo across India and globally.

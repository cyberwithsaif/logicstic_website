Empowering India's Critical Sectors

At Ashtam Global Logistics, we specialize in end-to-end logistics solutions for the sectors that power and build India — from massive power transformers and oil & gas equipment to automotive parts and pharmaceutical supplies.

Different industries face vastly different logistical challenges. We bring specialized equipment, deep regulatory knowledge, route expertise, and hands-on operational experience tailored to each sector's unique requirements.

Our core strength lies in handling over-dimensional, heavy, and project cargo with precision, safety, and timely execution — serving Defence, Power, Hydro Projects, Oil & Gas, Energy, Infrastructure, Renewables, and Heavy Engineering industries.

Core Heavy Industry Expertise

Deep sector knowledge and specialized equipment for India's most demanding industries

Power & Energy

Power & Transformers

Undisputed experts in power transformer transportation — from 1 MT to 240 MT. We move generators, stators, turbines, and transmission equipment to remote substations and power plants across India.

  • Heavy hydraulic axle trailers for transformers up to 240 MT
  • Route survey, RTO permits & police escort coordination
  • DISCOM & utility shifting for overhead clearance
  • Substation positioning & multi-modal logistics
Infrastructure & EPC

Infrastructure & EPC

Building India's highways, bridges, dams, and cities requires an uninterrupted supply chain. We partner with leading EPC firms to deliver heavy construction equipment, structural steel, and precast elements on JIT schedules.

  • JIT deliveries synchronized with EPC project milestones
  • Transport of cranes, excavators & earthmoving machinery
  • Structural steel, precast & modular components
  • Clients include Ashoka Buildcon Ltd.
Oil & Gas

Oil & Gas

Logistics for oil & gas involves navigating hazardous environments and handling ultra-heavy refinery equipment. We provide robust transport for pressure vessels, columns, reactors, rigs, and pipeline materials across remote terrains.

  • Strict HSSE compliance and zero-incident execution
  • Pressure vessels, columns & ODC process equipment
  • Delivery to remote, constrained & offshore support zones
  • Hazardous cargo certified handling

More Industries We Specialize In

Renewables

Renewables & Energy

The green energy transition demands specialized logistics for oversized wind blades, nacelles, tower sections, and solar farm equipment. We offer precision solutions built around the unique dimensions and fragility of renewable energy components.

  • Extendable trailers for ultra-long wind turbine blades
  • Nacelle, tower & hub transport to remote wind sites
  • High-volume solar panel logistics for project rollouts
  • Clients include Suzlon Group
Iron, Steel & Engineering

Iron, Steel & Heavy Engineering

Steel plants, foundries, and heavy engineering manufacturers rely on consistent inbound raw materials and outbound finished goods logistics. We handle hot-rolled coils, structural sections, heavy castings, forgings, and fabricated equipment.

  • Steel coils, plates & structural sections transport
  • Heavy castings, forgings & press equipment
  • Plant-to-plant and mill-to-site deliveries
  • Clients include Sareen Steels & Triumph
Auto, FMCG & Consumer

Automobile, FMCG & Consumer

From automotive OEM supply chains to FMCG distribution networks, we provide reliable, time-bound freight solutions. Our flexible FTL and PTL options scale with production volumes and seasonal demand fluctuations.

  • OEM & Tier-1 supplier inbound/outbound logistics
  • Tyre, rubber & auto-component transport
  • FMCG, pharma & consumer goods distribution
  • Clients include Riddhi Mattresses
